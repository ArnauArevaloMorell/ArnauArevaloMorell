## About

I'm a **Mathematical Engineering in Data Science** student at Universitat Pompeu Fabra, interested in **quantitative finance, machine learning, mathematical modelling and data-driven decision making**.

I enjoy taking problems that are difficult to structure and turning them into quantitative systems that can actually be tested, optimised and deployed.

Currently focused on:

* 📈 Quantitative finance & systematic trading
* 🤖 Machine learning & statistical modelling
* 📊 Financial time series & regime detection
* 🧮 Optimisation & stochastic modelling
* 💻 Building data-driven applications

## Selected Work

### Quantitative Finance

At **Santander CIB Global Markets**, I worked on quantitative models for credit derivatives, building Python-based pricing and risk systems for CDS, CDS indices and index default swaptions.

Outside of work, I compete in algorithmic trading competitions and build systematic strategies using statistical analysis and time-series modelling.

**IMC Prosperity**

* 🥇 1st in Spain — Prosperity4
* 🏆 Top 0.2% globally
* 🥉 3rd in Spain — Prosperity3

### Data Science & Machine Learning

Interested in building systems that combine multiple quantitative models rather than relying on a single approach.

My current TFG explores a system where different models analyse financial time series from different perspectives, detect changing market regimes and dynamically learn how much weight to assign to each model.

### Software Engineering

Previously worked on an **LLM-powered system for OTC derivatives**, parsing FpML, validating trades against the ISDA CDM and generating natural-language trade descriptions.

I also enjoy building things outside finance, from algorithmic systems to software projects and hackathons.
